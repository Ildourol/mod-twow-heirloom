# Heirloom Module System & Complete Armory Guide

A comprehensive manual and quick-reference guide for **mod-twow-heirloom** on Turtle WoW (1.18.1 / 1.12.1).

Every gear slot, weapon type, and jewelry piece features **three distinct specializations**:
1. **Tank (Vanguard)**: Maximized Stamina, Strength/Agility, and Armor/Defense.
2. **Healer (Grace)**: Maximized Intellect, Spirit, and Healing/Restoration power.
3. **DPS (Slaughter)**: Maximized Attack Power, Agility/Strength or Spell Damage.

- **Authentic Lore Inscriptions** ??" evocative flavor text on every item.
- **Dynamic 1??"60 Stat Scaling** ??" scales continuously as the character levels up.

## 4. Full 8-Slot Mathematical Level 40 Armor Morphing

The system features an automatic 1-to-1 armor adaptation system:
- **Warriors & Paladins (Plate <-> Mail)**:
  - Below Level 40: Plate Tank (`99111x`) morphs to Mail Tank (`99121x`), Plate Healer (`99112x`) morphs to Mail Healer (`99122x`), Plate DPS (`99113x`) morphs to Mail DPS (`99123x`).
  - At Level 40+: Morphs automatically to Plate and learns the Plate Mail armor skill.
- **Hunters & Shamans (Mail <-> Leather)**:
  - Below Level 40: Mail Tank (`99121x`) morphs to Leather Tank (`99131x`), Mail Healer (`99122x`) morphs to Leather Healer (`99132x`), Mail DPS (`99123x`) morphs to Leather DPS (`99133x`).
  - At Level 40+: Morphs automatically to Mail and learns the Mail armor skill.

## 5. Client Cache Note (Fixing White Item Color)

If an item appears with a white name or outdated stats:
1. Completely close the World of Warcraft client.
2. In your WoW client directory, delete the **`WDB` folder** (specifically `WDB\itemcache.wdb`).
3. Relaunch the client and log in. The client will query fresh templates from the server and render the items in their **Legendary Orange** quality with all stats and lore descriptions.
